Appendices

Appendix H — Index of Works

Continue in Reference

This appendix lists every novel, story, play, poem, film and television series that this book names, from the Introduction to Appendix G, with its author, director or creator, the year it first appeared, and where it appears. Short stories are in quotation marks and other works in italics, as in the chapters. Each place is given by chapter and section, and the section names are links. The works are in alphabetical order of title, ignoring A, An and The, under letter headings.

A reader who knows a work can use the index to find every place where this book discusses it. Because the analyses often describe how a work ends, a reader who has not yet read or seen a work can also use it to see which sections to leave until afterwards. Criticism and books on craft are cited where they are used and are not listed here; the Reference volume gives each chapter’s sources.

The year is that of first publication, first performance or first broadcast, in the original language. A novel first published complete in parts over more than one year, as a serial or in volumes, is given the span from the first part to the first edition as a book; a television series is given the span of its broadcast, open-ended if the series continues.
